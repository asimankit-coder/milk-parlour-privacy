MY MILK PARLOUR

Privacy Policy

Milk Collection System — Made easy and secure
Effective Date: 4 September 2026
Important: This Privacy Policy explains how information is stored, used, shared, protected, retained, and deleted when you use the My Milk Parlour application.

1. Introduction

My Milk Parlour is a milk collection management application designed to help manage customer information, milk collection records, payment information, user access, reports, and related business records.

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling information responsibly. This Privacy Policy describes the information that may be stored by the application and how it is handled.

2. Information Stored by the Application

The application may store the following information entered by the user:

  • User name and mobile number.
  • WhatsApp number and email address.
  • User address and account-related information.
  • Password-related information used for authentication.
  • Customer names, mobile numbers, WhatsApp numbers, and addresses.
  • Milk collection records, including quantities, rates, dates, and related entries.
  • Payment records and related transaction information maintained in the application.
  • Reports and backup information generated from the application's records.

3. Local Storage

The application's operational records are stored locally on the device using the application's local database. The application is designed to work with these records on the user's device and does not require a continuously connected online server for its core milk collection functions.

Because information is stored on the device, users should protect their device with appropriate screen-lock and security measures.

4. How Information Is Used

The information stored in the application is used to provide its intended functions, including:

  • Managing users and application access.
  • Maintaining customer records.
  • Recording daily milk collection.
  • Calculating and maintaining milk and payment records.
  • Generating daily, monthly, and other reports.
  • Creating backups and restoring application data when requested by the user.

5. Sharing of Information

The application does not provide customer or business records to third parties for advertising or sale.

The application may provide a user-initiated sharing facility for reports or backups. When the user chooses to share a file, the selected third-party application or service may receive the information contained in that file. Such sharing is controlled by the user and is subject to the privacy practices of the selected third-party application or service.

6. Password and Account Security

Passwords are handled using password-related security mechanisms rather than being intended to be displayed as plain text. Users should keep their login credentials confidential and should not share them with unauthorized persons.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should also maintain appropriate security on the device where the application is installed.

7. Backup and Restore

My Milk Parlour provides backup and restore functionality so that users can preserve and recover application records. Backup files may contain personal and business information stored by the application.

Users are responsible for choosing a secure location for backup files. If a backup is shared with another application or service, that service's own privacy policy may apply to the shared file.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may remain in the application for as long as it is required by the user for business and record-keeping purposes, unless the user deletes it or removes the application data.

Customer records and related milk history may be deleted through the application's available deletion functions. Users should create an appropriate backup before deleting information if they may need the records later.

9. Children's Privacy

My Milk Parlour is intended for business and milk collection record management and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly seek to collect information from children for purposes unrelated to the application's intended use.

10. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used within the application to protect stored information, including controlled access to application functions and password-related protection. However, the security of the device, operating system, backups, and any third-party service used to share files is also important and remains the user's responsibility.

11. Internet and Third-Party Services

The core application records are designed to operate locally. The application may interact with Android system functionality, such as the device's sharing facility, when the user explicitly requests an action such as sharing a report or backup.

Third-party applications or services selected by the user may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. Users should review those policies before sharing sensitive information.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the application, applicable requirements, or privacy practices. The updated version will be made available through the applicable privacy-policy page and, where appropriate, within the application.
